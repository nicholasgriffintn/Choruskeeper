# Asset generation record

All original raster artwork was created with the built-in image generation workflow in `stylized-concept` or `logo-brand` mode. The reference screenshots were not supplied as edit targets; the prompts deliberately establish a separate world, silhouettes, palette, and material treatment.

## Sky Isles

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: portrait iOS game environment background. Original fantasy game background for Choruskeeper: a vertical vista of floating garden islands suspended in a vast twilight sky, connected by delicate ribbons of golden sound and tiny glowing motes. Layered indigo sky, distant cloud sea, a welcoming mossy island with brass chimes, oversized bell-shaped flowers, and a circular stone resonance dais. Polished storybook paper-diorama illustration with hand-painted gouache texture and cut-paper depth. 9:16 portrait with calm space for UI. Midnight navy, peacock teal, mist blue, warm brass, and coral highlights. No text, logos, dragons, eggs, forest UI, recognisable characters, or watermark.

## Bramblebeat

Full body, expressive oval eyes, short russet legs, compact readable silhouette. Polished hand-painted gouache and layered paper-cut game sprite with tactile edges. Deep moss, fern green, russet, aged brass, and cream. Genuine transparent background; no text, logo, dragon features, wings, or watermark.
